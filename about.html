Who I am CIANJUR / INDONESIA

I’m Irham Khairul Kalam — a Statistics graduate who moved from analyzing data into building systems around automation and AI.

I’m interested in what happens when data stops being a report and starts becoming a system.

Today I like working where data, software, automation, and real operational problems meet.
